Indoor baseball and softball training in Clovis, California

Elite Performance Center is an indoor, air-conditioned baseball and softball training facility at 906 Barstow Ave, Suite A, Clovis, CA 93612. It serves players from Clovis and Fresno with batting lanes, pitching machines, private lessons, HitTrax, and space for teams.

Training options

Baseball batting lanes

Indoor baseball pitching machine lanes for individual practice or small groups. $40 per hour for up to four players, $10 per additional player.

Softball batting lanes

Softball pitching machine lanes for individuals and teammates. $40 per hour for up to four players.

Private lessons

One-on-one baseball and softball instruction with an experienced coach. Contact the facility about availability and rates.

HitTrax

Batting practice with real-time HitTrax data and analytics to understand swing mechanics. Currently listed at $85 per person, $20 per additional person, up to four.

Teams and groups

Single-lane and whole-facility rentals, with up to six cages for facility bookings.

Rates

Lane rental: $40 per hour. Single membership: $250 per month, one hour of hitting per day. Team membership: $600 per month, whole facility one day per week for two hours.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reserve a lane?

Use the online scheduler to see availability and reserve your session. Contact the facility directly for lessons or group arrangements.

Can I train with friends?

Yes. Standard lane rentals allow up to four players, with a fee for each additional player. For larger groups, ask about facility rentals.

Do I need to be on a team?

No. Individual players, families, and groups are all welcome to book time.
